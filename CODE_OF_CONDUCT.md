# Community conduct

Be respectful and focus on the work. Questions, corrections, and disagreement are welcome. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, spam, and publishing someone else's private information are not.

Keep reports and examples synthetic. Do not post task contents, account details, credentials, or private conversations. Follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md) for vulnerability reports.

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive discussions, or restrict participation. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and applied consistently. Good-faith mistakes can be corrected.

Use GitHub's Report content control for abuse. For a routine moderation concern, open an issue that identifies the relevant discussion without repeating private information or harmful content. This is a volunteer project; there is no guaranteed response time.
